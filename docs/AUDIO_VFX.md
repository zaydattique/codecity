# Audio and VFX

Source and verify per-file licenses, no GTA audio. Prioritize footsteps synced to animation, engine pitch from vehicle state, doors, dialogue, city ambience and terminal/equipment effects. Spatial buses and distance attenuation, variation, subtitles, offline narration optional, accessibility and actual settings controls. Rain, spray, brake lights, wetness and electrical effects derive from world state; budget particles, shadow/reflection cost and avoid hiding bad assets. No audio/VFX implemented or measured yet. Master directive sections 21–23.
