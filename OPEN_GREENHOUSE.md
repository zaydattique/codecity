## How the objects were made
Blender Python generates tapered curved roots and stem geometry, leaf meshes with shape keys for unfolding, a procedural soil material with noise and bump, and keyframed 3D water droplets. Lighting is rendered with Cycles. Poly Haven CC0 plant and pot source assets were acquired separately; they are not yet integrated in the macro scene. No AI-generated video is used. The plant model is illustrative, not species calibrated. The actual image quality has not been visually approved.
